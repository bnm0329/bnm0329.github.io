Mr Bnm Uploader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Mr Bnm Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Mr Bnm YouTube channel to upload and manage that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and no other users can sign in to it.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses these YouTube scopes:

  • youtube.upload – to upload the channel's own videos and thumbnails.
  • youtube.force-ssl – to set video details, captions and thumbnails on the channel's own videos.
  • yt-analytics.readonly – to read the channel's own view statistics (for example views and average percentage viewed) to improve future videos.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to read its own statistics.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer as private environment variables. They are never shared.
  • The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and it does not access data of any other YouTube or TikTok user.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok

The app also posts the same videos to the owner's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API, with the owner's permission (TikTok Login Kit). It uses the scopes user.info.basic (to show which account is connected and its posting options), video.upload and video.publish (to upload and publish the owner's own videos). It reads no other TikTok data and no data of other TikTok users. Use of TikTok is also governed by the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the app can no longer access the channel, and stored tokens are deleted.
